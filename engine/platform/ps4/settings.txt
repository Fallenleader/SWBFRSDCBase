# Aurek (PS4, GNM through PS4GL_GNM) settings: /data/Aurek/settings.txt

# Controls (the DualShock 4; the PSP game's button layout with the sticks split: left moves, right aims).  L = L1 or L2,
# R = R1 or R2, START = OPTIONS, SELECT = the touch pad (SHARE belongs to the system).
#   SELECT tapped: next level (a new match).  SELECT + L: the level before.  SELECT + R: figures / debug text on / off.
#   SELECT + SQUARE: play (a soldier in a conquest match with bots) / free camera.  SELECT + CIRCLE: the model gallery.
#   On foot: CROSS fire, SQUARE secondary (the thermal detonator: thrown, sticks, 3 s fuse), R hold lock-on, L tap jump, L hold
#            sprint (starts after 0.1 s held, forward only; stamina lasts 6.7 s, then L must be let go before sprinting again), d-pad UP get in / out of a vehicle (a teammate bot driving it hands it over) or man a turret of your side.
#            CIRCLE roll (the way the left stick points: forward dive, left, right, back), TRIANGLE zoom (steps through the
#            weapon's zoom levels, then back out).
#   Ground vehicles: left stick throttle / steer, right stick aims, CROSS fire, SQUARE secondary, L hold boost, UP get out.
#            Walkers (AT-ST) answer the stick 0.47 s late, as the game's do, and finish their stride when let go.
#   Flyers: left stick pitch (forward = nose down; AK_INVERT_Y_VEHICLE / AK_INVERT_Y_SPACE flip it) and turn / bank; on the ground R takes off; flying, R
#            speeds up toward the cruising speed and L slows toward the stall speed (the speed stays when let go); in space,
#            tap R then hold it again within 0.5 s to boost to top speed; CROSS fire, SQUARE secondary;
#            touching down slowly and level lands.  Keeping an
#            enemy in the sights locks on (locking / LOCKED) and a tracking secondary then guides itself.
#   Heroes: when your side's hero is offered to you, d-pad UP takes it, DOWN declines (30 s); a lightsaber hero strikes with CROSS.
#   Free camera: left stick move, right stick look, L / R down / up, hold CROSS to move faster.
#   Gallery: d-pad left / right model, up / down clip, right stick turns, left stick zooms.
# button layout: psp (the game's, above) or legacy (R fire, L lock, CROSS jump, TRIANGLE get in)
